# Reading list: how interviewers score working with coding agents

Articles on how AI-assisted / agentic coding interviews are actually graded, grouped by how useful they are. Relevant to the Agentic Engineering interview (Ryan & Jonny) and, for the last section, the AI System Design interview (Karan & Saumya).

## Most useful: written by interviewers or from interviewer interviews

- **[Canva: AI Interview Success, An Interviewer's Inside Guide](https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/ai-interview-success/)**
  Written by one of their interviewers. Names three red flags: "AI Showcase," "Feature Marathon" and "Hands-Off." Includes prep tips such as having your project structure ready.
- **[Hello Interview: AI-enabled coding, Introduction](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/ai-coding/overview/introduction)**
  Based on interviews with engineers who went through these rounds at Meta, Shopify, LinkedIn and Canva. Covers the four graded areas (problem-solving, control, verification, communication), failure modes such as silent stretches and narrating after the fact, and a step-by-step workflow.
- **[Hello Interview: AI coding hub](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/ai-coding)**
  Practice sessions, the Fundamentals lessons (planning, driving the AI, verification) and company guides.

## Good overviews

- **[GreatFrontEnd: AI-Assisted Coding Interviews](https://www.greatfrontend.com/blog/ai-assisted-coding-interviews-for-frontend-developers)**
  Which companies run these rounds and how, including Google's "AI fluency" criteria.
- **[Formation: What AI-Assisted Coding Interviews Actually Score](https://formation.dev/blog/ai-assisted-coding-interviews)**
  Makes the point that catching and fixing AI mistakes is one of the strongest signals.
- **[PracHub: How to Prepare Without Letting AI Take Over](https://prachub.com/resources/ai-assisted-coding-interviews-how-to-prepare-without-letting-ai-take-over)**
  A six-stage workflow, starting with getting oriented in the problem.
- **[IGotAnOffer: AI-assisted coding interview](https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/ai-assisted-coding-interview)**
  Advice from a Meta engineering manager and a Microsoft principal engineering manager.

## From the hiring side (what interviewers are told to watch for)

- **[Developer Toolkit: Hiring and interviewing for agentic engineering](https://developertoolkit.ai/en/org/hiring/)**
  Scores what the agent *can't* supply: a clear spec, a verification plan that would catch a wrong result, and so on. Has a good example contrasting two candidates, where only the one who wrote a failing test "did the job."
- **[CodeSignal: Agentic Interviewing](https://support.codesignal.com/hc/en-us/articles/38841637349015-How-can-I-use-Agentic-Interviewing-in-my-recruiting-process)** and **[Codility: Claude Code in interviews](https://support.codility.com/hc/en-us/articles/49075175492497-Claude-Code)**
  Show that interview platforms now support Claude Code and Cursor directly, and that prompts may be recorded for review.
